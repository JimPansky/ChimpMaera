# PANSPHAIRA Community Code of Conduct

Contributors, reviewers and maintainers must collaborate respectfully, focus
criticism on the work and make participation free from harassment,
intimidation and discrimination.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, clear
attribution, respect for privacy, honest disclosure of uncertainty and prompt
correction of mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, retaliation and
disclosure of confidential security reports.

Maintainers may warn, restrict or remove a participant whose conduct harms the
community or project. A maintainer involved in a complaint must recuse from
its review. Enforcement should record only the minimum information needed for
the decision and appeal.

Use the private maintainer contact published alongside the distribution for
conduct reports and appeals. If no private contact is available, do not
disclose sensitive personal or security information publicly.
