# The Zoo Field Guide

## Applying the ChimpMaera Canon in Practice

The Canon helps people prepare and improve tools and integrations that can act
on real systems. It is a growth path, not a certification scheme or an entry
exam.

An unfinished tool can still be useful. It may enter the Zoo, be tried, and
develop through use and community contributions even when its safeguards or
evidence are limited. What matters at the foundation is honesty: known
capabilities, authority boundaries, evidence, gaps, and operating limits must
not be misrepresented or materially concealed, and authority must never expand
silently.

## Start with the real context

Describe what the tool can actually do, what external effects it can produce,
who or what acts, and where authority is enforced. Then select the Canon
principles that are relevant to those capabilities and risks. A practice does
not become mandatory merely because another, more capable tool needs it.

If a capability or hazard is absent, explain why the corresponding practice is
not applicable. Formal proof is not required for admission or use. If a
relevant safeguard or evidence item is missing, describe it as a visible
development or trust gap and identify the next useful improvement.

## Keep capability separate from permission

A tool, credential, template, or request may make an action technically
possible, but it does not authorise the action. The Owner defines a visible,
versioned authority envelope. New actions and integrations start without
runtime authority and remain inactive until they are explicitly enabled under
a known Policy.

Grant only the scope that is needed. Bind every material effect to the Actor,
target, operation, content, scope, and Replay identity. Before the effect,
present an effect-free Plan and a human-readable diff. Bind Approval to the
exact action, and revalidate it in trusted code at the effect boundary.

Credentials remain at that boundary, outside the Agent's planning path.

## Prove outcomes without overstating trust

Success requires authoritative Readback bound to a Receipt, not transport
acceptance. Missing identity, Policy, Approval, target state, or Readback fails
closed. Missing Evidence means uncertainty, not authority, proof, or confirmed
success.

Replay must not duplicate an effect. If an effect may have occurred but the
outcome is unknown, reconcile it against durable Replay state before retrying.
Treat Revocation, Rollback, and Cleanup as separate operations with separate
authority, contracts, and evidence.

Evidence is a trust offer, not an admission ticket. A maintainer may provide
tests, reviews, signatures, or operational evidence to help an operator decide.
The operator still determines fitness for its own context and risk tolerance.
Evidence becomes binding when someone makes a concrete claim: the claim must
match the version, scope, and observations actually tested.

## Improve one meaningful gap at a time

When the tool evolves, preserve the applicable invariants or document an
explicit migration, its risks, compatibility, evidence, and fallback. Reassess
which practices have become applicable as capabilities and risks change.

Close the next most important gap, disclose the remaining limits, and invite
feedback and contributions. Useful systems may improve rapidly; narrow or
less-used systems may remain modest and honest. Neither admission nor utility
requires artificial completeness, a maturity score, or a universal profile.
