# Reference Asset Usage

The media under `assets/reference/` is provided as optional, replaceable
reference material for experimentation and derivative video work. A job may
use, replace, modify, or omit every bundled example. No character, voice,
visual identity, logo, or other reference asset is required by the renderer.

The machine-readable inventory is `assets/reference/reference-assets.json`.
When a job declares a reference asset, its path, accepted status, and SHA-256
are checked fail-closed. This integrity check does not make the asset
mandatory.

Bundled reference media is preserved under the conservative boundary in the
root `MEDIA-LICENSE.md`. No CC BY, CC0, TTS model, source-input,
output-rights, logo, word-mark, trademark, endorsement, or broader media grant
is asserted until the exact candidate receives rights review. Third-party
components and separately identified material retain their own license or
rights boundaries. Apache-2.0 does not grant trademark rights or permission to
imply official endorsement.

Users remain responsible for their configuration, modifications, outputs,
rights clearance, and safe use under applicable law and license terms. No
warranty is provided. This responsibility statement does not waive, restrict,
or override applicable law.

Community improvements and replacements are welcome. When a published video
materially uses a community production path or contributor work, the practical
intent is to reference that path and credit relevant contributors where
appropriate and with their consent. This is a non-guaranteed attribution
practice, not a promise of promotion or endorsement.
